The workflow

One task, from idea to merged. Each step is one command. Nothing changes status without your yes, and you review the work in small pieces on GitHub.

Brainstorm and draft. The draft lives in tmp/ (not in git). On your yes, it moves to plans/<task>/ (in git) on a new branch, with a PROGRESS.md log.

On GitHub, stages 1 and 2Task issue opened, then the task branch and the plan link in the issue. Each plan update is committed to the task branch.

Finalize

/finalize-plan

Claude checks that every step is ready to build: one commit, or a big step with sub-steps. It marks gaps and proposes the stops.

then planned

Build

/implement-next

Claude builds and reviews each step, up to the next stop: one reviewed commit per step.

one run per stop

On GitHubA reviewed commit and a ticked box per step.

Close
